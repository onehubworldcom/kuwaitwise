SAVINGS GUIDE

Saving in small steps when your expenses are not perfectly predictable

A simple approach for building a savings habit without relying on a single fixed monthly number.

Create three savings buckets

Separate a small emergency buffer, planned irregular expenses and a longer-term goal. You do not need to fund all three equally at the start.

Use a minimum and a bonus amount

Choose a small contribution you can usually manage, then add more in stronger months. This can be easier to maintain than an unrealistic fixed target.

Review, do not punish

If you miss a target because of a real expense, update the plan instead of abandoning it. The purpose is to make your money more visible.
