SMART COMPARISON

Before you sign: a reusable checklist for recurring services

Use the same questions when comparing a rental, internet plan, membership or other recurring commitment.

Seven questions to ask

  1. What is the real monthly cost?
  2. Are there one-time setup, deposit or equipment charges?
  3. How long am I committed?
  4. How can I cancel or change the service?
  5. What is included—and what costs extra?
  6. What happens if there is a problem or dispute?
  7. Can I keep a written copy of the offer and terms?

Marketing can help you discover options, but your decision should be based on the exact plan or agreement you are considering.
