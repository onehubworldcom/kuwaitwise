NEWCOMER GUIDE

New to Kuwait: a practical first-weeks checklist

A calm, practical order for organizing the basics. Requirements differ by situation, so use this as a planning framework rather than an official checklist.

Start with the essentials

  1. Keep key documents organized. Maintain secure copies and check which originals are needed for your own situation.
  2. Confirm where you will stay. Before committing, compare the location, commute, utilities and total monthly cost.
  3. Build a first-month budget. Separate one-time setup costs from recurring expenses.
  4. Plan connectivity. Compare coverage, data needs and contract terms before choosing a service.
  5. Map your regular journeys. Work, school, groceries and medical visits can change which area feels practical.

Do not try to solve everything on day one

Newcomers often make expensive choices by rushing. Start with temporary or flexible arrangements where possible, collect the facts you need, then compare the options that affect you every month.

Useful rule: prioritize decisions that are hard or costly to reverse, such as a long rental commitment or a recurring service contract.

Use a personal comparison sheet

For each option, write the monthly cost, one-time fees, travel time, cancellation terms and the one reason it suits your household. This makes the final choice easier to explain and revisit.
